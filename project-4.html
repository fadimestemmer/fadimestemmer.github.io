Metabolism, Metaproteomics, and Protease Activity into the Depths and Across the North Atlantic

This projects contains several lines of metaproteomics investigations of samples collected during two cruises conducted in the North Atlantic between the Bermuda Atlantic Time Series (BATS) and North-East US Shelf Long-Term Ecological Research Station (NES-LTER) aboard the R/V Atlantic Explorer in March (AE2504) and September (AE2520).

Metaproteomic Analysis and Protease Activity in Surface Waters Across Seasons

Samples collected using Underway sampling during both cruises were analyzed for active microbial community composition, nutrient stress biomarkers, protease activity biomarkers (proteases, bacterial secretion system proteins), and related to physical measurements of temperature, salinity, fluorescence and inorganic nutrients. Collaborations include a high-time-resolution multi-omics analysis at BATS and LTER.

Depth Profiles of a North Atlantic Metaproteomic Transect using the AUV CLIO

Samples were collected using the Automatic Underwater Vehicle (AUV) CLIO and analyzed for active microbial community composition, nutrient stress biomarkers, protease activity biomarkers (proteases, bacterial secretion system proteins) across depth, space, and time. Subprojects include the investigation of a cold-core Eddy contrasting Eddy core and Eddy edge metaproteomic, transcriptomic, and metabolomic profiles.

Purine-mediated Cryptic Nitrogen Cycling Among Ocean Microbes Revealed by Metaproteomics-Stable Isotope Probing

Here we conducted stable isotope incubations using 15N and 13C labeled Adenine variants on microbial communities collected from seawater at BATS at 15 m (surface), 110 m (DCM), and 250 m (depth). The isotope incorporation in main marine oligotrophic microbial representatives across depth will reveal fine-scale dynamics and cryptic nitrogen and carbon cycling between those cells.
