Secreted Proteases in Marine Particle Attached Microorganisms and Correlation with C, N, and Zn Cycling

  • This project combines metaproteomic analyses and protease activity measurements in a variety of marine particulate isolates and investigates proteome and secretome (extracellular proteome) changes when grown on protein as sole carbon or nitrogen source as well as on varying dZn concentrations.
  • In the first part of this work I investigated extracellular protease activity and related changes in secreted and cellular proteomes in the model marine microbe Ruegeria pomeroyi DSS-3 when grown on protein as sole nitrogen source. This work was presented at the 2025 Gordon Research Conference for Chemical Oceanography and released as my Master Thesis Chapter. The manuscript is in final stages of preparation and will be submitted to mBio. Reach out to me via email to learn more about the project. Find the Github repository here.
  • Future iterations will look at isolates from particulate organic matter collected during an expedition to the Subtropical North Pacific (CLIOMZ cruise) in 2023. More to come soon!
